## What we never receive
The Strikta engine and Strikta Pro run on your machine and send us nothing: no telemetry, no usage data, no licence checks, none of your stored memory. We are not a controller of the memory data on your machine. If you configure alert notices to ntfy, Telegram or an email webhook, those notices go from your machine to the service you chose, not to us.
