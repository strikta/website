# Withdraw from your Strikta Pro purchase

Strikta Pro is "Sold through Link": Sold through Link, LLC ("Link"), a Stripe affiliate, is our reseller and merchant of record through Stripe Managed Payments. You can withdraw with us, Mightreya AB, whose details are in the form below, and we refund through Stripe; you can also ask Link support for a refund (Strikta Pro Licence Terms, section 6.5).

- **Who can withdraw.** If you are a consumer in the EU or the UK, you can withdraw from your purchase of Strikta Pro within 14 days of the day you bought it, without giving a reason. You keep this right when you start using Pro; we never ask you to give it up.
- **How.** Email support@strikta.dev, ideally from the address you bought with, saying that you withdraw and naming your purchase (your order or receipt number, or the date and email address you bought with). You can also use the form below, by email or by letter, or tell us clearly in any other way. The form is optional.
- **In time.** It is enough to send your withdrawal before the 14 days end.
- **We confirm.** We confirm by email, without undue delay, that we received your withdrawal.
- **We refund everything.** We refund everything you paid, through Stripe to your original payment method, within 14 days of being told. The refund costs you nothing.
- **Your licence file ends.** The licence file that payment bought ends and your subscription is cancelled. The file's identifier is added to the revocation list in later releases, so a release installed after the refund will refuse it; the copy you already installed keeps running, but you must stop using Pro under that file.

The 14-day refund in our refund policy is at least as good, so in practice you need only ask. Full terms: Strikta Pro Licence Terms, section 6, and the Strikta Pro refund and cancellation policy.
