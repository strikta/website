# Strikta stays AGPL

What we commit to, and exactly how far the commitment reaches.

## The commitment

1. Every release of the Strikta engine that we have published under AGPL-3.0-only stays available under AGPL-3.0-only, for ever. A licence already granted to you under AGPL cannot be taken back, and we will not try.
2. Whenever we license an outside contribution we have merged under any licence other than AGPL-3.0-only, we must also license it under AGPL-3.0-only. This is a condition in our Contributor License Agreement (section 2.3(c)); if we break it, our licence to that contribution fails. Section 6.3 makes anyone who takes over these rights accept the same condition in writing. It does not oblige anyone to keep publishing the engine, but copies already published under AGPL stay under AGPL (item 1).
3. We intend to publish every future engine release under AGPL-3.0-only. Nothing in the engine is or will be held back for paying customers: every operation of Strikta Pro uses the engine's documented API.

## What the contributor agreement is for

It lets Mightreya AB license the engine, including merged contributions, under two other licences as well:

- a commercial licence for businesses that embed Strikta in a product or run it as a service without AGPL's obligations (COMM-LICENSE); and
- the licence included with Strikta Pro for one person's own use.

It also lets Strikta Pro, which is closed source, ship an unchanged copy of the engine's admin API description.

## What this commitment does not cover

Read this before you rely on it.

- Item 3 is a statement of intent, not a contract. Code written by Mightreya AB itself is ours to license. A future owner of the project could publish new code it writes under other terms. It could not withdraw items 1 and 2.
- Strikta Pro, the commercial licence and the Pro engine licence are not AGPL, and this commitment does not make them AGPL.
- The Strikta name and logo are covered by our trademark policy (TRADEMARKS.md), not by AGPL.

If we ever change item 3, we will announce it in this file and in the release notes before the first release it affects.
