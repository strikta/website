# Strikta Pro support period

Manufacturer: Mightreya AB, org. no. 559174-7992, VAT no. SE559174799201, registered office (säte) Huddinge, Sweden, postal address C/o Mightreya AB, Kivra: 559174-7992, 106 31 STOCKHOLM, Sweden, security@strikta.dev.

For each release of Strikta Pro we handle vulnerabilities and provide security updates free of charge until the end of [month and year: at least five years after the release date] (the "support period"). Security updates are published as releases marked as security releases and can be installed with any licence that has not been revoked, including lapsed licences. Each security update stays available for at least ten years after its release, or for the rest of the support period if longer.

We set this period from the expected use time of a self-hosted developer tool, the support periods of comparable tools, and the support of the components Strikta Pro depends on (Python, Starlette, the Strikta engine). This is recorded in our technical documentation.
